Invitation cards

About ShaadiTemplate

ShaadiTemplate is a free maker for Indian wedding invitations. Every design is a real card layout, not a stock photo with text dropped on top, so names, dates and venues sit where a card printer would put them. You pick a design, replace the sample couple with your own families, and download the finished card. There is no account to create and nothing to install.

How it works, step by step

+
  1. Choose a design by event (Save the Date, Ring Ceremony, Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception or Baby Shower), language, and the god or sacred symbol your family uses at the top of the card: Ganesha, Radha Krishna, Shiv-Parvati, Ram-Sita, Balaji, Gel Mataji, Umiya Mataji, Ashapura Mataji, Shrinathji, Swaminarayan, Ek Onkar, Bismillah, the Jain Ahimsa hand, a cross, or a simple floral motif.
  2. Fill in your details. Each card comes with sample wording written for that ceremony, so you edit rather than start from a blank page. The bride's and groom's names, their parents, the date, time, venue and the family members who are inviting guests all have their own fields.
  3. Download and share. Save a tall 1080 by 1920 pixel image for WhatsApp and status updates, or a 5 by 7 inch PDF at print resolution to hand to your local press.

Six languages, typed the easy way

+

Cards come in English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi and Tamil. Each language uses fonts made for its own script, so a Gujarati kankotri looks like a Gujarati kankotri and a Tamil card keeps proper Tamil letterforms. You do not need an Indian-language keyboard: phonetic typing lets you type shubh vivah in English letters and get शुभ विवाह. You can also mix scripts on one card, for example an English invitation with a Devanagari shlok at the top.

One card for every ceremony

+

Indian weddings run over several days, and many families send a separate invitation for each function. ShaadiTemplate has designs for each one. Haldi and Mangala Snanam cards use turmeric yellows and marigold. Mehendi cards lean on henna greens and peacock teal. Sangeet invitations are built for an evening of music and garba. Wedding cards carry the full muhurat, the baraat or jaan arrival and the pheras, while reception cards are simpler and more formal. A multi-page wedding card can also include a page listing every function, so guests get the whole programme in one file.

Frequently asked questions

Is ShaadiTemplate free?

Yes. You can design, edit and download invitations without paying and without signing up.

Where is my invitation saved?

In your own browser, on your device. Nothing you type is uploaded to our servers. If you clear your browser data or switch phones, the draft does not follow you, so download a copy when you are done.

Can I print the card at a shop?

Yes. Choose the print format and download the PDF. It is laid out at 5 by 7 inches, a common invitation size, at roughly 360 dots per inch, which is enough for sharp offset or digital printing.

Can I change the language after choosing a design?

Yes. Every card can switch language once it is open, and the sample wording changes with it.

Do my guests need an app to open the invitation?

No. The WhatsApp version is an ordinary image, and the PDF opens on any phone or computer.

Can I make a wedding website with RSVP?

Yes, for free. Choose a wedding website design, fill in your functions and photos, and publish. Guests open one link, see every event and reply; you see who is coming.
