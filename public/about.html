About ShaadiTemplate

ShaadiTemplate helps families make wedding invitations that look as grand as the wedding itself. Pick a design, fill in your families' names, dates and venues, and download images for WhatsApp or a print-ready PDF for the press.

Invitations come in English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi and Tamil, for every event from Haldi and Mehendi to Sangeet, the wedding and the reception. Phonetic typing lets you write in your own script with an English keyboard.

There is no login. Your draft stays in your own browser.

Why we built it

Getting a wedding card made usually means a trip to the press, choosing from a catalogue of designs, then several rounds of corrections over the phone while the printer retypes names in a script they may not read. Digital invitation apps skip the press but tend to offer generic designs, English only, or watermarks unless you pay. ShaadiTemplate tries to sit in between: designs that look like a proper Indian wedding card, in your own language, that you can finish yourself in an evening and take to any printer or send on WhatsApp.

What goes into each design

Every design is laid out like a real card, with the invocation, names, family lines, dates and venue placed where families expect them. Sample wording is written separately for each ceremony and each language rather than translated word for word, so a Gujarati kankotri reads like one and a Tamil card uses the terms Tamil families use. Each script is set in a typeface designed for it.

Guides

We also write practical guides on invitation wording, traditional card layouts and printing, based on the questions families ask while making their cards.
