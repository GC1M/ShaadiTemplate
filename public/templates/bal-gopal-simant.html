About this design

Bal Gopal is a five-page simant card on a sage green wash, copied from a printed Gujarati set. Red berries and green vines hang from the top of every page, and banana and coconut palms with a small bird frame the bottom, where each page has its own painting.

The cover has સીમંત સંસ્કાર inside a thin gold ring and laughing baby Krishna seated on a pink lotus. Page 2 shows a couple portrait with the wife in a maroon gown. The next three pages follow little Radha and Krishna: seated with the flute between two white cows, playing among flowers, and riding a bicycle together on the closing page, which ends with a painted Thank You.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in deep green, large and easy to read on a phone. It suits Gujarati families who want a playful Krishna theme across several pages. The sample names Chamunda Maa as the kuldevi; it is typed text you can change.
