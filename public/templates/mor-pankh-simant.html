About this design

Mor Pankh is a two-page simant card on warm cream with faint leaf shadows, copied from a printed Gujarati card. A blue-green peacock stands at each top corner with its long tail falling down the side, and the bottom is a dark green jungle with white jasmine.

The cover has a painted આમંત્રણ title in green, then “માતૃત્વ” and “સીમંત સંસ્કાર” with small blue flourishes, above baby Krishna asleep on a bed of peacock feathers. The second page closes with a painting of a mother and father holding their newborn.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in navy and olive green. It suits Gujarati families with a Shrinathji or Umiya Mataji tradition: the top line carries both beside the Ganesha invocation, and all of it is typed text you can change.
