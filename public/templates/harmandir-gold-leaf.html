About this design

A night-blue velvet card. Along the bottom stands Harmandir Sahib, the Golden Temple, drawn in gold leaf: the gilded central dome with small domed kiosks at its corners, the arched lower storey, and the causeway with its lamp posts leading across the water. Below it the dark sarovar holds a faint reflection of the temple, crossed by gold ripple lines. A frieze of small cusped arches runs along the top edge, and a gold hairline double frame goes round the card.

The same artwork sits on all six pages, so the whole set reads as one card. Headings and the couple's names are in gold and the rest of the text is white, which stays clear against the deep blue on a phone. The cover carries an Ek Onkar emblem in a gold ring under the opening line.

It suits Sikh and Punjabi families holding an Anand Karaj at a gurdwara who want the Golden Temple on their card. For the events around the wedding, Kalire Gold Chunni is a Punjabi ring ceremony card that also opens with Ek Onkar, and Phulkari Bagh Mehndi is a Punjabi mehendi card with no religious line.

Sample wording on this card

The cover opens with ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ and the title ਆਨੰਦ ਕਾਰਜ. The sample follows a Sikh wedding morning: milni at 8:30 and the Anand Karaj at 10:00 at the gurdwara, with the parents written as Sardarni and Sardar and ਜੀ after their names. Replace the sample names, gurdwara and phone number with your own in the editor.

The other five pages are ਸ਼ੁਭ ਮੌਕੇ (a Sukhmani Sahib path, an evening of giddha and bhangra, and a short verse from the children), ਸ਼ੁਭ ਘੜੀ (the milni, the lavan in the presence of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji, and lunch from 12:30), ਆਨੰਦ ਕਾਰਜ (the letter, which ends with ਸਰਬੱਤ ਦਾ ਭਲਾ), ਉਡੀਕਵਾਨ (the family names, with the nanka side and the daughters of the house) and ਡਿਜੀਟਲ ਸੱਦਾ (the closing message asking guests to reply OK).
