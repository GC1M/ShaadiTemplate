About this design

An ivory card dressed like a Paithani sari. The lower third is a tall magenta pallu woven with two pairs of large gold peacocks facing gold lotus sprays, a row of small lotus buds beneath them, between bands of peacock green and small gold diamonds. Across the top is a narrow green and gold vine border, thin magenta lines run down both sides, and small gold asharfi buttis, the coin motif of Paithani, are scattered over the body.

All six pages share the same border and pallu. Titles are in magenta and the text is in dark brown, with names in gold on the letter and closing pages. The cover has a small Om roundel, the emblem used on this site for Lord Ganesha cards, between the Ganesha shloka and the title.

It suits Marathi families who want a traditional lagna patrika with the look of a bride's Paithani. For the haldi, Thipka Rangoli Haldi is a Marathi card with the same Ganesha line.

Sample wording on this card

The cover opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and the shloka वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय, then शुभविवाह and a line from the bride's parents with आपणास सस्नेह निमंत्रण. The bride is written with चि.सौ.कां. and the groom with चि., each with their parents' names, joined by समवेत. Below come the date in Marathi numerals, the varat and mangalashtak times, a sample mangal karyalay in Pune and आपले नम्र (the hosts) with a phone number.

The other five pages are मंगल प्रसंग (muhurtamedh, a sangeet and garba evening and a short verse from the children), मंगल सोहळा (varat, मंगलाष्टक व अक्षता, sahabhojan and venue), शुभमंगल (the letter), आपल्या प्रतीक्षेत (the family names, with आजोळ and लाडक्या लेकी) and डिजिटल निमंत्रण (the closing message asking guests to reply OK).
