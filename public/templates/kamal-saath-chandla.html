About this design

Kamal Saath is a one-page painted chandla vidhi (sagai) card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. The page is dusty rose watercolour inside a fine rose frame, with filigree scrolls in every corner.

A painted Ganesha seated on a pink lotus crowns the top. In the lower left a watercolour couple walk away hand in hand, the groom in an ivory kurta and the bride in a blush pink lehenga with flowers in her hair, and tall magenta lotuses bloom in the lower right.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in deep maroon. It carries the same letter as Toran Kamal on a softer, more romantic page. The sample names Ashapura Mataji as the kuldevi; that line is typed text you can change.
