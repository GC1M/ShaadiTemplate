About this design

A dark rust-brown card, the colour of hand-woven khaddar, with a deep band of phulkari bagh embroidery across the top and another across the bottom. The bands are packed with darned silk diamonds in golden floss, each diamond stitched in fine parallel lines so it catches the light, with small magenta and white diamond hearts set in them. A dotted gold line marks where each band meets the cloth, and small darned diamonds in faint gold floss carry on across the middle behind the text.

In the middle, a small round gold emblem with a lotus sits above ਮਹਿੰਦੀ ਦੀ ਰਸਮ in large gold Gurmukhi. The names are in the same gold, joined by ਸੰਗ, and the date, time, venue and the hosts under ਦਰਸ਼ਨ ਅਭਿਲਾਖੀ follow in a softer gold. There is no invocation line and no deity, as the gallery's floral label says.

It suits Punjabi families sending out a mehndi or ladies' sangeet invite on WhatsApp, where the phulkari bands read as Punjab at a glance. For the rest of the wedding, Kalire Gold Chunni is a Punjabi engagement card and Harmandir Gold Leaf is the six-page Anand Karaj card, both opening with Ek Onkar.
