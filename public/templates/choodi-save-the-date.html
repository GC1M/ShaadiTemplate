About this design

A soft lilac card with a thin gold frame. At the bottom, a stack of bridal choodiyan rests on its own soft shadow: lac red, green glass, purple and gold kundan bangles, the kundan ones set with white and ruby stones in gold collets. A loose red bangle and a loose kundan bangle lie on either side. A string of coloured glass beads runs inside the frame. At the top, a small set of bangles seen from above sits between two gold rules.

A small Om roundel, the emblem used on this site for Lord Ganesha cards, sits under the invocation. The title and names are set large in deep plum, and the date is spaced out with dots so it reads at a glance. There is no venue line, only the city, with a note that the full invitation will follow.

It suits Hindi-speaking families who want to fix the date with guests early, before the full wedding card is ready. Send Gota Patti Rani Pink later as the Hindi wedding card.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and the title तिथि याद रखें, then the line कृपया इस शुभ तिथि को हमारे लिए सुरक्षित रखें. The bride and groom are written with चि. before their names and joined by संग. Below come the date written as 21 . 11 . 2027, the note निमंत्रण पत्र शीघ्र (invitation to follow), a sample city and the निवेदक (hosts) with a phone number.

Replace every name, date and address with your own before you share it. The card can also be switched to English, Gujarati, Marathi, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same artwork.
