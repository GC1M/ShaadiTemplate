About this design

A sangeet card for a night out: a deep purple sky with a few stars and a thin lagoon-blue neon line across the top, and at the bottom a striped sunset sinking into the sea under a glowing pink horizon, with a low skyline across the bay and palm silhouettes on the sand at both sides.

The title is set in tall condensed capitals in sunset yellow, the names in coral and the headings in flamingo pink, with the Ganesha emblem above the title. The rest of the text is warm sand on the dark sky.

It suits couples planning a beach or rooftop sangeet, a cocktail night or a Bollywood party evening. It matches the Vice Nights wedding website, an open-world game style site that goes from golden hour to neon night as guests scroll.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with || Shri Ganeshaya Namah || and the title Sangeet Night, then the line "Join us for an evening of music and dance celebrating" before the bride's and groom's names joined by "with". Below come the date, an evening start time, a sample palace venue and an RSVP line with the hosts and a phone number.
