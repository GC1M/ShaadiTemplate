About this design

Each page is framed like a leaf from an illustrated Kalpasutra manuscript. An ivory panel sits inside wide ultramarine borders, edged with a vermilion rule, with a gold vine and rows of white dots running down the sides. Across the top are three lotus roundels in vermilion and gold. At the bottom, a pair of white elephants with red saddle cloths face a lotus roundel in the centre, the way they flank a scene in the old paintings.

The same frame holds all six pages. The text is deep blue, with the names in red, and the cover carries a round Jain emblem with the Ahimsa hand under the opening line.

It suits Gujarati Jain families who want a lagna patrika with Jain imagery rather than Ganesha. Patola Ikat Sagai is a Gujarati engagement card from the same set, and Sandhya, Van Utsav and Neel Gulab are painted Gujarati kankotris.
