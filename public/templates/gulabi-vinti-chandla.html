About this design

Gulabi Vinti is a one-page chandla vidhi (sagai) card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. Pink watercolour blossoms and green leaves frame all four corners of a soft cream page, with a sprig of white baby's breath on the right edge.

A small maroon Ganesha emblem sits at the top. Two gold rings sit between the couple's names, small maroon rules divide the sections, and at the bottom a pair of engagement rings, one set with a diamond and green stones, rests on a gold cushion with tassels.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in maroon and rose. It suits families who want one soft, floral card with every detail on a single page. The sample names Brahmani Mataji as the kuldevi; that line is typed text you can change.
