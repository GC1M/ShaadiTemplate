About this design

A deep green jharokha arch with a thin gold outline stands on cream paper, hung with strings of orange and yellow marigolds. At the bottom an illustrated couple in matching green kurta and lehenga sit on a carved wooden bench with polka-dot cushions, the bride showing her henna, between bunches of white jasmine and peonies.

Mehndi is painted in a bold gold script, with widely spaced white capitals above and below, the names in a gold brush script and a row of calendar, clock and pin icons for the date, time and venue. There is no god or religious line, so it suits Hindu, Muslim and mixed families alike.

Sample wording on this card

It opens with Please join us for a Mehndi celebration, then "In honour of" and the couple's names between two flower dividers, then the day, time and venue in three columns and "Let's celebrate together!" above a small gold heart. Replace the sample names, date and address with your own in the editor. The card can also be switched to Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same layout and artwork.
