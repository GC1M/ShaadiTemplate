About this design

Harit Mandap is a one-page chandla vidhi (sagai) card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. Sprigs of eucalyptus and small gold flowers grow in from the corners and edges of a plain white page. The original was a 5 x 7 inch print card; here it is widened to phone shape, with more paper in the middle for the text.

A small gold Ganesha emblem sits at the top, and two gold rings sit between the couple's names. In the lower left the couple sit together under a mandap of greenery and lace, the groom in a grey suit and the bride in a sage green lehenga with white flowers.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in deep green with gold. It suits families who want a light, modern card. The sample names Khodiyar Maa as the kuldevi; that line is typed text you can change.
