About this design

A white marble card with soft grey veins running across it, decorated like the inlaid stone of a Mughal tomb. At the bottom, a cusped arch of white marble is cut into a panel of black inlay edged in gold, with an inlaid flower in each spandrel. Inside it stands a large lapis blue vase of carnelian red and lapis blue tulips on malachite green stems and leaves. A broad black and gold inlay border goes all round the card, set with small carnelian flowers.

The card opens with Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim in dark green and a round emblem with a crescent and star, then Walima Reception in large green serif. The bride and groom's names are in deep blue with their parents underneath, joined by an ampersand, and the date, time, venue and RSVP are in dark grey.

It suits Muslim families holding a Walima, or a Nikkah if you change the title and time. The inlay is flowers and a vase only, with no figures, in the manner of the Taj Mahal's marble. Marble and inlay keep it formal and quiet, which suits an evening dinner at a hotel or banquet hall. For a reception in Hindi with Ganesha, look at Aaina Mahal Mirror Work.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens in the editor with this text. Change any line, or switch the card to another language.

The sample is a Walima: Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim, the title Walima Reception, a line in which both sets of parents "request the pleasure of your company at the Walima of" the couple, the bride and groom as D/o and S/o their parents joined by &, a Monday date at 8:00 PM onwards, a placeholder palace hotel on Lake Pichola, Udaipur, and an RSVP from both families with a phone number. For a Nikkah, change the title to Nikkah and put in the time of the ceremony.
