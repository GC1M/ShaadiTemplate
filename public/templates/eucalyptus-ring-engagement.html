About this design

Eucalyptus Ring is a one-page painted engagement ceremony card, copied from a digital card. The page is textured white paper with a eucalyptus branch across the top.

A wreath of watercolour eucalyptus and fine gold leaf outlines holds a gold-edged frame for the title. At the bottom blue-grey succulents, white roses and pink butterflies surround a pair of gold engagement rings with a posy of pink roses.

The title and names are set in Pinyon Script and the date in Cormorant Garamond, in olive ink.

Sample wording on this card

The title “Engagement Ceremony” sits inside the gold frame. Below it come the couple’s names in script, the date as day, month and year between rules, and the time, venue and address. Replace every name, date and address with your own before you share it. The card can also be switched to Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same page and artwork.
