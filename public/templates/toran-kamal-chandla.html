About this design

Toran Kamal is a one-page painted chandla vidhi (sagai) card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. A cream arch with a scalloped top stands between carved ivory pillars on a sage green wall, under a toran of mango leaves and white and pink flower strings hung with brass bells.

A small pink Ganesha sits at the top of the arch. At the foot of the page the couple stand side by side, the groom in a bottle green sherwani and the bride in a dark green floral lehenga with a mint dupatta, with pink lotuses rising in both corners.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in plum and rose red. It suits families who want the couple on the card and a short, formal letter. The sample names Ashapura Mataji as the kuldevi; that line is typed text you can change.
