About this design

A deep indigo card like a night sky, with a soft glow in the middle and a fine copper frame round the edge. Copper and brass bunting hangs across the top, with small mirror dots on the flags. At the bottom stands a copper garbo, the perforated earthen pot carried in the garba circle, with a lit diya on its lid. Light pours out of the rows of holes in the pot and spreads into rings of small golden dots around it.

The text sits in the dark space above the lamp. A copper Om roundel, the emblem used on this site for Lord Ganesha cards, sits under the invocation, and the title, names and headings are in warm copper with the rest in pale peach.

It suits Gujarati families holding a sangeet sandhya with garba and raas the night before the wedding, and it works for a Navratri-style garba evening too. For the morning before, Pithi Bandhani Mirror Work is a Gujarati pithi card with the same Ganesha line.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with ॥ શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ ॥ and the title સંગીત સંધ્યા, then the line સંગીત અને ગરબાની રંગત માણવા પધારશોજી. The bride and groom are written with ચિ. before their names and joined by સંગ. Below come the date with the weekday, a night time (રાત્રે ૮:૦૦ કલાકે), a sample party plot in Surat and the નિમંત્રક (hosts) with a phone number.
