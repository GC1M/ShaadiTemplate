About this design

Meadow Vows is a one-page painted engagement card, copied from a digital card. The page is cream watercolour paper with hanging eucalyptus sprigs at the top, two sparrows and two white butterflies in flight.

Along the bottom a watercolour couple walk away into a green meadow, the groom in a navy suit and the bride in a pale blue gown, with a rabbit in the grass and white magnolias in the corners.

The couple’s names are set in a flowing Pinyon Script, with the small capitals and the date in bold Cormorant Garamond, all in olive ink.

Sample wording on this card

It opens with a line asking guests to celebrate as the couple exchange rings, then the couple’s names in script, a request for the guest’s company at the engagement, the date set out as day, month and year between rules, and the time, venue and address. Replace every name, date and address with your own before you share it. The card can also be switched to Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same page and artwork.
