About this design

Ivory paper with a band of Patan patola double-ikat weave at the top and the bottom. Each band has a red ground with a row of large stepped diamonds outlined in black, filled with saffron and white steps round a green heart, and small white crosses between them. The edges of the motifs are stepped and feathered the way ikat blurs in the loom, and a black-and-white chevron selvedge runs along the band edges. Narrow patola borders of small ikat diamonds run down both sides, and a faint red ikat pattern covers the ivory ground, so the text sits inside the cloth.

The text is dark brown, with the names in deep red. A small round emblem with Om sits under the Ganesha line at the top.

It suits Gujarati families who want a sagai card that draws on Patan patola, the silk often given to the bride. Jain Kalpasutra Lagna Patrika is a Gujarati wedding card from the same set, and Pithi Bandhani Mirror Work and Garbo Lamp Raas Night cover the pithi and garba.
