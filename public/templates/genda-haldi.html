About this design

A soft butter-yellow card framed in marigolds. Strings of orange genda flowers swag across the top edge in loops, caught up with bunches of green mango leaves, and single strands of marigolds run straight down both sides to the bottom. At the foot of the card sits a brass urli filled with turmeric paste, among loose marigolds and scattered leaves.

A small round gold emblem with a lotus sits above Haldi Ceremony in large rust-brown serif. The names are in a lighter brown, joined by with, and the date, time, venue and RSVP follow in plain dark type. There is no invocation or shlok, so the card is not tied to one religion.

It suits a Haldi or pithi morning sent in English to friends and family of any background. If you want guests in yellow, add a dress code line under the venue. For a Marathi Haldi with Ganesha, look at Thipka Rangoli Haldi, and for a Gujarati pithi, Pithi Bandhani Mirror Work.
