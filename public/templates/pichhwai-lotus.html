About this design

A pale celadon green card in the style of a Nathdwara pichhwai. Along the bottom is a lotus pond: pink lotuses in full bloom, buds on tall stems and broad green leaves floating on deep blue water. A blue band runs across the top, and from it hangs a garland of small pink lotus buds strung on gold threads with gold beads. Fine gold lines run down both sides.

The emblem is a flute with a peacock feather in a gold-ringed roundel, the Radha-Krishna emblem used on this site. The title and the names are set in deep plum and the rest of the text in dark grey, leaving the space between the garland and the pond open and calm.

It suits Hindi-speaking families, especially Krishna devotees and Pushtimarg families, holding a sangeet or a music evening before the wedding. For the wedding itself, Gota Patti Rani Pink is a six-page Hindi wedding card.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with ॥ श्री राधे कृष्ण ॥ and the title संगीत संध्या, then the line संगीत और नृत्य भरी शाम में आप सादर आमंत्रित हैं. The bride and groom are written with चि. before their names and joined by संग. Below come the date, an evening time (सायं 7:30 बजे से), a sample palace venue in Udaipur and the निवेदक (hosts) with a phone number.
