About this design

Neel Gulab is a soft blue kankotri with room for your own pictures. Two of its eight pages are photo pages: page 3 shows the couple inside a gold ring wreathed in blue and white roses, and page 7 is a full-page photo. Open either photo page in the editor to upload your own picture, then drag it and zoom to choose the part the frame shows. It shows through the artwork, so the gold ring and roses still sit on top. Until you do, a sample couple fills them. Your photo stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

The rest of the card is copied from a printed watercolour set. The cover is a white square on a pale blue wash, framed by blue roses, lilac buds and eucalyptus leaves, with a gold namaste emblem above the family name. The inner pages are white with fine line-drawn flowers in the corners. The letter page carries a picture of Rajrajeshwari Butbhavani Mataji between શુભ and લાભ, the family page has line drawings of a bride and groom, and the closing page returns to the roses with a second gold ring.

The headings, such as મંડપ મુહૂર્ત, જાન પ્રસ્થાન and દિવ્ય આશિષ, are cut from the original card in plum. Text is set in Hind Vadodara in plum and dark grey, with the functions in two columns. The Mataji picture is part of the artwork, so check that it suits your family before you pick this card.
