About this design

Cream paper with a large Maharashtrian thipka rangoli, the kind drawn round a grid of dots, rising from the bottom edge. Its petals are kumkum red, haldi yellow and leaf green, each with a smaller leaf inside, and rings of coloured dots spread out from it over its own dot grid. Across the top runs a border of four-petal dot motifs in red and yellow under a thin red rule.

The text is in deep kumkum red, with the title and names in a bold Devanagari face. A small round emblem with Om sits under the Ganesha line at the top.

It suits Marathi families holding a morning haldi at home or in a mangal karyalay. Paithani Peacock Pallu is the Marathi wedding card from the same set, and Genda Marigold Haldi is an English haldi card with marigolds.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and the title हळदी समारंभ, then हळदीच्या मंगल समारंभास आपण अवश्य यावे. The bride's name takes चि.सौ.कां. and the groom's चि., joined by समवेत. The time is सकाळी १०:०० वा., the sample venue is a mangal karyalay in Sadashiv Peth, Pune, and the reply line is headed आपले नम्र. Replace the sample names, venue and phone number with your own in the editor.
