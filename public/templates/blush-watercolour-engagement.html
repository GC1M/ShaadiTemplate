About this design

Warm blush and cream watercolour paper framed by dusty pink and ivory roses, dried palm fans and soft leaves. A small black Ganesha emblem sits at the top, and at the bottom a watercolour couple: the bride in a pale gown with a red bouquet, the groom in a charcoal suit kissing her hand.

The names are in a rose copperplate script, the day and time in soft pink boxes beside the date, and the rest in a clean sans serif. The design comes from an animated invitation; this is its finished still.

Sample wording on this card

The sample reads Together with our families, we invite you to the joyful engagement ceremony of, then the names, a Friday in May at 12 PM, the blessing line and a farm venue. Change the names, date and venue in the editor. The card can also be switched to Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same layout and artwork.
