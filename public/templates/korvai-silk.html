About this design

The card is drawn as a length of Kanchipuram silk in arakku red, with fine woven lines that give it a slub sheen. Across the top is a deep brass zari border cut into a row of temple-tooth triangles (the rettai pettu pattern), finished with a thin peacock-blue korvai line where it meets the red. Narrow zari borders run down both sides. At the bottom is a zari pallu woven with rows of mango buttas in red and a row of annam (swans).

The same silk and borders frame all six pages. Headings and names are in pale gold and the rest of the text is cream, and the cover has a Balaji emblem with the Vaishnava namam in a gold ring.

It suits Tamil families who want a wedding card that looks like a bridal silk saree, and especially those who open with Lord Venkateswara. For the engagement, Kolam Kaavi Nischayathartham is in Tamil with the same Balaji line, and Thanjavur Gilt Mandapa is a Tamil reception card.

Sample wording on this card

The cover opens with ஓம் நமோ வேங்கடேசாய, then திருமண அழைப்பிதழ் as the title. The bride's name takes சௌ. and the groom's சி., the muhurtham is 7:30 to 9:00 in the morning, and the sample venue is a kalyana mandapam in Mylapore, Chennai. Replace the sample names, venue and phone number with your own in the editor.

The other five pages are மங்கல நிகழ்வுகள் (pandhakkal muhurtham, a sangeet evening and a verse from the children), திருமண நிகழ்வுகள் (mappillai azhaippu at 5:30, the muhurtham with மாங்கல்ய தாரணம், and the wedding lunch from 11:00), திருமண அழைப்பிதழ் (the letter), வருகையை எதிர்நோக்கும் (the family names, with the mother's side and the daughters of the house) and மின்னஞ்சல் அழைப்பு (the closing message asking guests to reply OK).
