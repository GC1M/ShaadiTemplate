About this design

Neel Sagar is a two-page painted chandla vidhi (sagai) card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. White drapes tied back with bows open on a calm sea at dusk, with blue roses in the top corners and gold birdcages filled with blue flowers hanging on both sides.

On the cover a gold hexagon wreath with blue leaves floats above the couple's names and date. Below, on a wooden deck, the couple sit on a cream sofa banked with blue roses, the groom in a blue kurta slipping a ring onto the bride's finger and the bride in a blue anarkali. Page 2 carries a fine blue Ganesha emblem at the top, the full invitation with small rules between sections, and the same couple, smaller, at the foot.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in navy. It suits families who want a calm blue card with the ring exchange itself on the cover. The sample invokes Swaminarayan and Mahant Swami Maharaj; that line is typed text you can change.
