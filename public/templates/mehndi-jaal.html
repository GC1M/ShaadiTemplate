About this design

Blush paper with a large henna mandala hanging from the top edge, only its lower half showing: rings of petals, hatched leaves and fine dots, drawn in henna brown in three line weights so it looks like a cone design rather than a stamp. A smaller mandala rises from the bottom edge. A copper hairline frame with a border of tiny henna dots runs round the card.

Mehendi Ceremony is set in a light, tall serif and the names in a bolder serif, all in henna brown, with plenty of open paper between the two mandalas. There is no invocation, god or sacred symbol, so the card can go to guests of any background.

It suits a relaxed English mehendi afternoon, sent on WhatsApp to friends. Phulkari Bagh Mehndi is a brighter mehendi card in Punjabi, and Genda Marigold Haldi is an English haldi card with no religious line.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with Mehendi Ceremony and the line "Join us for an afternoon of henna, music and colour with", then the two names joined by "with". The sample is a Saturday afternoon from 3:00 PM, at a placeholder hotel in Udaipur, with an RSVP line for both families. Replace the sample names, venue and phone number with your own in the editor.
