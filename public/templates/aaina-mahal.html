About this design

A plum velvet card, lighter in the middle and darker at the edges. A tall jharokha arch fills the card, its pointed top and straight sides built entirely of hand-cut mirror shards of different sizes and angles, set in dark lead and tinted with what they reflect: plum, silver, gilt and the odd bright glint, like the sheesh mahal work in a Rajasthani palace. A row of mirror facets also runs along the bottom edge. Outside the arch, round sheesh mahal medallions of mirror petals set in gilt plaster sit in the two upper corners, and small star-shaped glints catch the light here and there.

The text sits inside the arch in white, with the title and names larger. A small round emblem with Om stands under the Ganesha line at the top.

It suits Hindi-speaking families hosting an evening reception, where the silver on plum looks dressed up without much colour. Gota Patti Rani Pink is the Hindi wedding card from the same set, and Pichhwai Lotus Pond is a Hindi sangeet card.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and the title प्रीतिभोज. The host line has both families asking for your presence at the newly married couple's reception, the names take चि. and are joined by संग, and the time is रात्रि 8 बजे से. The sample venue is a hotel in Udaipur and the reply line is headed निवेदक. Replace the sample names, venue and phone number with your own in the editor.
