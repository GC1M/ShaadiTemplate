About this design

A warm turmeric-cream card, lighter in the middle, with a band of saffron bandhani across the top and another across the bottom. Each band is covered in tiny white tie-dye dots and has a scalloped inner edge. Set along it are four large red chandrakhani medallions, each ringed with white dots round a round silver abhla mirror, and between them sit small silver mirror discs that catch the light like the mirror work on a pithi chunni.

The middle of the card carries a faint ground of red chaubundi, four tied dots to a diamond, kept light so the Gujarati text stays easy to read. A small Om roundel in grey-green, the emblem used on this site for Lord Ganesha cards, sits under the invocation, and the title is set large in deep green. The couple's names are in maroon and the date, time, venue and hosts follow in dark brown.

It suits Gujarati families sending a separate card or WhatsApp image for the pithi, the haldi ceremony held at home or at a party plot a day before the wedding. For the other functions, Garbo Lamp Raas Night is a Gujarati sangeet and garba card and Patola Ikat Sagai is a Gujarati engagement card, both with the same Ganesha line.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with ॥ શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ ॥ and the title પીઠી પ્રસંગ, then the line પીઠીના મંગલ પ્રસંગે પધારશોજી. The bride and groom are written with ચિ. before their names and joined by સંગ. Below come the date in Gujarati numerals with the weekday, a morning time (સવારે ૧૦:૦૦ કલાકે), a sample party plot in Surat and the નિમંત્રક (hosts) with a phone number.
