Save the date cards

A save-the-date goes out months before the wedding card, often before the venue or the full schedule is fixed. Its job is simple: tell people the date and the city so they can book leave and travel. Keep it short, the couple's names, the date and the place, and leave the details for the invitation. For families with relatives abroad, it is the card that matters most, because flights and visas need months of notice.

Most save-the-dates are sent digitally, so the phone format is the one to download first. If you send a printed one too, use the same design as your wedding card so the two feel like a set.

What to write on a save the date

Three lines are enough: the names, the date and the city, then a note that the invitation will follow. Leave out the venue and the function times until they are fixed. These are ready to copy; the editor fills the same shape for you.

English: Save the date. Riya and Karan are getting married. 14 February 2027, Ahmedabad. Invitation to follow.

Hindi: तिथि याद रखें। रिया और करण का शुभ विवाह। 14 फ़रवरी 2027, अहमदाबाद। निमंत्रण पत्र शीघ्र।

Gujarati: તારીખ યાદ રાખજો. રિયા અને કરણના શુભ લગ્ન. 14 ફેબ્રુઆરી 2027, અમદાવાદ. કંકોત્રી ટૂંક સમયમાં.

Send it three to six months ahead, or earlier for guests who need visas. Most families send the phone image on WhatsApp first and the printed wedding card later.
