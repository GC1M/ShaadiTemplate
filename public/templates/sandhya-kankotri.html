Sandhya Pink Dusk Kankotri: painted Gujarati kankotri

  • Ceremony: Wedding (Vivah)
  • Language: Gujarati
  • God or sacred symbol: Lord Ganesha, Shiv-Parvati, Gel Mataji, Swaminarayan

About this design

Sandhya is built from a painted kankotri rather than drawn shapes, like Raj Mahel and Mor Bagh. Each of the six pages is a full-bleed painting on a pink dusk sky that fades to cream, with green vines hanging from the top edge and a painted scene filling the lower third. On the cover, Shiv and Parvati sit in meditation in front of a carved sandstone temple with a saffron flag, among trees, flowering shrubs and lotuses. The Vakratunda shlok and the calligraphed શુભ વિવાહ title are part of the painting, in dark green, and your text goes in the open sky below them.

The inner pages change scene. The functions page has Shiv and Parvati seated back to back among flowers, under a small Ganesha drawn into its title. The wedding-day page shows a baraat: the groom on a white horse, dhol and shehnai players, and a woman carrying a pot on her head. The letter page has the divine couple standing on a lotus in a lake with swans and a waterfall, the family page a doli procession, and the closing page Shiv and Parvati before the temple again. Some pages carry their own painted lines: ॥ શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ ॥ on the functions page, ॥ જય ગેલ માતાજી ॥ on the wedding-day page and ॥ જય શ્રી સ્વામિનારાયણ ॥ on the letter page. They are part of the artwork and cannot be edited, so check they suit your family before you pick this card.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara, the typeface of the original printed card, in plum and maroon, with the couple's names side by side around a small drawing of the couple. It suits Gujarati Hindu families who want a colourful, traditional kankotri to send on WhatsApp, where the paintings fill the phone screen. If you want the same pink palette without painted figures, Gulabi Kankotri is the quieter, drawn version.
