About this design

Jhoomar is a two-page painted chandla vidhi (sagai) card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. Crystal chandeliers hang from a canopy of white lilies and greenery, champagne drapes fall on both sides with strings of fairy lights, and a white flower aisle below is lined with lit pillar candles and silver disco balls.

The cover has only the couple's names with gold rings between them, above the painted couple dancing: the groom in a black bandhgala and the bride twirling in an ivory and gold lehenga. Page 2 keeps the same stage with a small Ganesha emblem at the top, the full invitation in the middle and a pair of gold rings on the aisle at the foot.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in olive and dark brown. It suits families planning an evening party who want a glamorous, ballroom look. The sample names Brahmani Mataji as the kuldevi; that line is typed text you can change.
