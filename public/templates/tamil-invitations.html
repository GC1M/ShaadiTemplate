Tamil kalyana pathirikai and invitation designs

A Tamil wedding invitation is a kalyana pathirikai (கல்யாண பத்திரிகை), or more formally a திருமண அழைப்பிதழ். It usually opens with an invocation to the family deity, gives the muhurtham as a window of time rather than a single hour, names the bride as சௌ. and the groom as சி., and closes with அன்புடன் அழைப்போர், the relatives who are inviting you. The engagement is the நிச்சயதார்த்தம் and the turmeric bath before the wedding the மஞ்சள் நீராட்டு.

The three designs below have Tamil as their own language, one each for the engagement, the wedding and the reception, and all three open with ஓம் நமோ வேங்கடேசாய. If your family prays to another deity, change the invocation line to your own. Korvai Silk Zari is a six-page wedding card; the other two are a single card. Any design can be switched to another language in the editor, and any other design can be switched to Tamil, which fills in the Tamil sample wording.

With phonetic typing on, type names in English letters (meenakshi, karthik) and the editor turns each word into Tamil script as you type. Press Esc to keep a word in English.

Korvai Silk Zari

Wedding (Vivah) · Tirupati Balaji

Arakku red Kanchipuram silk with a deep brass zari border of temple teeth, a peacock-blue korvai line, and a woven pallu at the bottom with a row of annam swans and mango buttas. The Tamil kalyana pathirikai is headed திருமண அழைப்பிதழ் and opens with ஓம் நமோ வேங்கடேசாய. Six pages, with the muhurtham window, the mappillai azhaippu and the mangalya dharanam.
