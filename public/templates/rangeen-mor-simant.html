About this design

Rangeen Mor puts the whole simant invitation on one page, copied from a printed Gujarati card. A thin gold frame sits on grey linen, with bright watercolour peacocks and wild flowers climbing in from the top left and bottom right corners.

Inside the frame a small gold Ganesha sits above સીમંત સંસ્કાર in gold script, cut from the original card. Two little babies, a girl and a boy, sit on either side of the mother-to-be's name, a balloon floats beside the venue, and a mother-to-be in a pink dress stands at the lower left.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in warm grey and gold, small enough to fit eight host names in two columns at the end. It suits families who want one card with every detail. The sample invokes Ranchhodraiji; that line is typed text.
