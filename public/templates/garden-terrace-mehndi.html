About this design

The cover is a bright blue sky with a tree draped in peach cloth and butterflies, above a terrace of green Mughal jharokha panels, a white diwan with lotus cushions and stacked planters. The couple stand in front, he in a teal kurta and she in a sage lehenga. Join us for a Mehndi celebration is set in a modern display serif across the sky.

Page 2 is soft sage paper under strings of fairy lights and trailing leaves, with macramé planters, a ladder with a watering can, succulents and a bed of leafy plants. The names are in a black copperplate script and the details in a light serif. The design comes from an animated invitation; this is its finished still. No god or religious line.
