About this design

Lantern Canopy is a one-page painted engagement card, copied from a digital card. The page is pale silver damask with glowing paper lanterns hanging in both top corners and a small gold Ganesha emblem at the top.

Below, a couple in matching ivory and gold, the groom in a short kurta and the bride in a lehenga, sit close together under a canopy of birch poles and peach and white flowers, with vases of roses on wooden stools at the sides.

The names are set in Marcellus and the rest in Cormorant Garamond, in dark olive.

Sample wording on this card

It opens with a warm invitation to celebrate the couple’s love and togetherness, then the couple’s names, the time and date, and the venue after “at”.
