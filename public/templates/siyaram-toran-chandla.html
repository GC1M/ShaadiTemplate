About this design

Siyaram Toran is a three-page painted chandla vidhi (sagai) card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. Every page is a carved ivory temple arch on parchment, with pink and white lotuses and peacock feathers at the foot.

The cover is crowned with peach and turquoise lotus petals and a painted Ganesha seated on a lotus between peacock feathers and two flutes. Shri Ram stands on the left with his bow and Sita on the right holding a lotus, and a brass kalash with a coconut, mango leaves and a diya sits between them. The cover also has a red monogram of the couple's initials. Page 2 hangs marigold and mango-leaf strings with brass bells inside a pink carved toran, with Krishna playing the flute carved on the pillars, and page 3 has a crown of lotuses and peacock feathers with Krishna and Radha carved on the pillars. Both inner pages carry a green Ganesha emblem drawn with an Om.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in warm brown. It is the most devotional card in the set, for families who want Ram and Sita on the cover and Radha-Krishna inside. The sample names Brahmani Mataji as the kuldevi; that line is typed text you can change.
