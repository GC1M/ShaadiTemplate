Engagement and ring ceremony invitations

The engagement goes by many names: sagai, chandla vidhi, roka, mangni, sakharpuda, Gol Dhana, nischayathartham. It is usually a smaller event than the wedding, hosted by one or both families, and it is often the first time the two families appear together on a card. Name both sets of parents, give the date, time and venue, and say whether the ring exchange is followed by lunch or dinner.

In Gujarati families the engagement is often called chandla vidhi (ચાંદલા વિધિ), and its card reads like a short kankotri: શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ and the kuldevi at the top, both sets of parents, the couple, the muhurat, the bhojan and the નિમંત્રક. Eight of the designs below are Gujarati chandla vidhi cards copied from printed ones.

Five painted English engagement cards come first: a meadow, a rose arch, a eucalyptus wreath with rings, a sage “Engaged” script and a lantern canopy with a Ganesha emblem.

If the engagement is close family only, a single phone-format image sent on WhatsApp is enough. For a larger party, print a small run on the same paper as your wedding card.
