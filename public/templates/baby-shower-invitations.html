Baby shower (simant) invitations

Simant, or સીમંત સંસ્કાર, is the Gujarati baby shower: a blessing ceremony for the mother-to-be, usually in the seventh month of her first pregnancy. Marathi families call it dohale jevan, North Indian families godh bharai, and Tamil families valaikappu. It is hosted by the husband's family, and the card is written in their name.

A simant card follows the order of a kankotri: an invocation and the family's kuldevi, the husband's parents, the couple written as ચિ. and અ.સૌ., the muhurat with the tithi and date, then the bhojan time, the venue and the hosts. Most are shared on WhatsApp, so every design below is a tall phone card, and each can also be downloaded as a 5 x 7 inch print PDF.

All eight designs are copied from printed Gujarati simant cards, with painted art of baby Krishna, Radha, Shiva and Parvati, peacocks, swings and teddy bears. Each opens in the editor with the original Gujarati wording, and can be switched to English, Hindi, Marathi, Punjabi or Tamil with the same pages and artwork.
