About this design

A warm black card in the style of 1920s art deco. A large champagne-gold sunburst fan rises from the bottom edge, its rays alternating long and short, solid and half-tone, around a small dark half-circle ringed with dots. At the top is a stepped crown made of thin parallel champagne lines that climb to a peak, and a fine champagne frame with stepped corners runs round the whole card.

A small round emblem with a lotus sits above Save the Date in large champagne serif. The names are in a high-contrast Bodoni-style serif joined by with, and the date is spaced out as 21 . 11 . 2027. The rest is in a clean sans-serif: "Formal invitation to follow", the city, and an RSVP from both families. There is no venue line and no religious line.

It suits couples planning a black-tie, cocktail or destination wedding who want a save the date that looks like an evening invitation rather than a festival card. For a brighter Hindi save the date with Ganesha, look at Choodi Bangle Save the Date.
