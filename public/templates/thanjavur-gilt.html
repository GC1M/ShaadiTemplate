About this design

The card is a deep emerald green, like the lacquered ground of a Thanjavur painting, and the whole text sits inside the raised gold frame those paintings are known for. Two fluted gold pillars run up both sides, studded with alternating ruby red and emerald green cabochons. They carry a rounded, cusped arch set with more stones and a row of small hanging pearl loops, and they stand on a jewelled gold plinth that runs across the bottom edge. The gold is shaded to look raised and polished, as gilded gesso work does.

Under the top of the arch is ஓம் நமோ வேங்கடேசாய and a round emblem with the Venkateswara namam, then வரவேற்பு in large gold letters. The names of the bride and groom are in gold with the Tamil prefixes செள. and சி., each followed by their parents' names, and joined by உடன். The date, an evening time of 7:00, a kalyana mandapam in Mylapore, Chennai, and the hosts under அன்புடன் அழைப்போர் are in white.

It suits Tamil families, especially Vaishnava and Balaji devotees, holding a reception after the muhurtham. The frame is ornament only, with no painted deity inside it, so the emblem and the invocation carry the devotion and the text has the whole middle of the card. For the wedding itself, Korvai Silk Zari is a six-page Tamil card with the same Balaji emblem, and Kolam Kaavi Nischayathartham covers the engagement.
