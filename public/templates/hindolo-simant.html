About this design

Hindolo is a four-page simant card in bright cartoon colours, copied from a printed Gujarati set. Each page has a clear blue sky, and the story moves from a big leafy tree to wisteria and back again.

The cover shows Yashoda guiding baby Krishna across the grass under a tree with a birdhouse, with સીમંત સંસ્કાર in red script painted on the original card. Page 2 has two peacocks in a meadow, page 3 baby Krishna on a swing with a toy elephant and rocking horse below, and the last page a carved wooden hindolo (cradle) hung with marigold ropes.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in navy and rose red. It suits Gujarati families who want a cheerful Krishna card with a lot of room for details. The invocation line names Khodiyar Maa and Mahakali Maa beside Ganesha; it is typed text you can change.
