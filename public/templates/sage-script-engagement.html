About this design

Sage Script is a one-page engagement card, copied from a digital card. It is the simplest of the set: soft white paper with a sage watercolour wash and seeded eucalyptus at the top, and a spray of round eucalyptus leaves at the bottom.

The word “Engaged” is set very large in Great Vibes script in sage green and ends in the original card’s painted heart flourish; the names are in the same script. The details are in small Jost capitals, like a modern printed card.

Sample wording on this card

It opens with “Engaged”, then “join us to celebrate the engagement of”, the couple’s names joined by a plus sign, the day, date and time, the venue and address, and an RSVP line with a name and phone number. Replace every name, date and address with your own before you share it. The card can also be switched to Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same page and artwork.
