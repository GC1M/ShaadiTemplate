About this design

A dusk-purple night sky over a marble terrace, with three crystal chandeliers and strings of fairy lights and pink flowers overhead, Corinthian pillars on both sides, cherry blossom and white roses. Below, gold candelabra, a white grand piano, a chequered floor and a groom on one knee slipping a ring on a bride in a coral lehenga. A small white Ganesha emblem sits under the middle chandelier.

The names are in a large coral copperplate script, the day and time in dark boxes on either side of the date, and the rest in a clean sans serif.

Sample wording on this card

The sample reads Together with our families, we invite you to the joyful engagement ceremony of, then the two names joined by And, a Friday date at 12 PM, a placeholder address and Please join us to bless the couple and celebrate their special day.
