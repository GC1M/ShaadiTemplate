About this design

Van Utsav is a forest kankotri: seven painted pages on a pale grey ground, copied from a printed family set, with green trees and palms growing up from the bottom of every page. The cover shows a carved white pavilion with a peacock perched on its dome and a parrot below, a palace behind it and willow leaves hanging from the top edge. The family name sits above it, and the couple's names sit on either side of a small painted couple.

Each inner page has its own scene. The invitation page has a golden swing between two peacocks, under a small Ganesha and the શુભ વિવાહ title cut from the original card. The functions page has a garba couple, a white cow on a green hill and swans in a lotus pond. The letter page shows the bride and groom at the sacred fire under a carved stone gateway, with a priest and musicians. The family page has a crowd of wedding guests posing under the same gateway, the elders' page a bright mandap canopy, and the closing page a palm forest with a painted namaste.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in dark green, with the functions laid out in three columns. It suits Gujarati Hindu families who want a light, leafy card that reads well on a phone screen. The letter invokes Rajrajeshwari Butbhavani Mataji as the kuldevi and the elders' page carries જય શ્રી સ્વામિનારાયણ; both lines are typed text, so you can change them to your own family's deities.
