About this design

Mor Bagh is a peacock garden kankotri, copied from a printed family set. Every page sits inside the same carved wooden lattice frame on a soft peach sky, with oversized pink lotus blooms, banana leaves and hanging brass bells at the edges. The cover has a peacock in the grass below a small garden pavilion, with Ganesha, a Sanskrit shlok and the family's name above the calligraphed શુભવિવાહ title, all cut from the original artwork.

The letter page has a pink Ganesha idol on a carved table in front of a white balustrade. The functions page shows Radha and Krishna on a swing at the top of a flight of steps lined with flower baskets, with a peacock on the path below. The wedding-day page has a carved stone mandap hung with red and white garlands, and the last page is an open garden for the family names and a closing message. The pages carry painted and typed invocations to the family's kuldevi, to Dwarkadhish and to Bholenath, so check they suit your family before you choose it.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in plum and olive, small and right-aligned in the name lists, the way the printed card was set. It suits Gujarati Hindu families, especially Krishna devotees, who want a soft, garden-themed kankotri with fewer pages than a full nine-page set.
