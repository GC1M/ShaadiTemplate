About this design

Gulabi Mamta is a simant (baby shower) card in three painted pages, copied from a printed Gujarati set. Every page has the same soft pink wash, hanging jungle vines with green palms at the top, and a garden of white and pink lilies along the bottom. A small red Ganesha emblem sits at the top of each page.

The cover sets the mood with two big words, માતૃત્વ (motherhood) and સીમંત સંસ્કાર, over a painting of baby Krishna asleep with his head on a butter pot. The second page carries the full invitation above a smiling mother-to-be in a lilac sari, and the last page names the hosts above little Krishna playing the flute beside Radha.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in deep maroon and olive. It suits Gujarati families who want a gentle, feminine card with Krishna in it. The sample names Mahalakshmi Mataji as the kuldevi; that line is typed text, so you can change it to your own family deity.

Page 3 lists the નિમંત્રક with phone numbers and ends with the family name and આપનું હાર્દિક સ્વાગત કરે છે. Replace every name, date and address with your own before you share it.
