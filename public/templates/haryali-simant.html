About this design

Haryali Mamta is a one-page simant card, copied from a printed Gujarati card. Pink frangipani and green tropical leaves frame the top corners of a soft cream page, and a painted mother-to-be in a pale green sari with a gold border fills the lower left, her hands on her belly.

A small maroon Ganesha emblem sits at the top. The bhojan and venue are set in a narrow column on the right, beside the painting, with small gold rules between them, as on the printed card.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in brown, gold and deep red. It suits families who want one elegant card with the mother-to-be at its heart. The sample invokes Ranchhodraiji; that line is typed text you can change.
