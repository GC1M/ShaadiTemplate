Soneri Jharokha Photo Kankotri: Gujarati kankotri with a photo cover

  • Language: Gujarati
  • God or sacred symbol: Lord Ganesha, Khodiyar Maa, Radha-Krishna, Bahuchar Maa, Swaminarayan
  • Format: WhatsApp image (1080 x 1920 px) and 5 x 7 inch print PDF

About this design

Soneri Jharokha opens on your own photo. The cover is a white jharokha arch edged in gold, set against a pale mandala with gold tassels hanging from the top and roses in the corners, and the couple's picture shows through the arch. Open the cover in the editor to upload your own picture, then drag it and zoom to choose the part the arch shows; the photo fades softly into the page below the faces, as on the printed card. Until you do, a sample couple fills it. Your photo stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere. Below the photo, the couple's names are set in an English wedding script with the date, under a gold plate carrying the family name.

The inner pages are copied from a printed set: a cream and peach watercolour wash inside a thin gold border, with grey and blush roses at the corners. Each page has a calligraphed Gujarati title in olive gold with a small painted deity in rust red. Ganesha appears on the invitation page and the functions page, Khodiyar Maa on the wedding-day page, Krishna with his flute on the letter page, Radha-Krishna on the family page and Bahuchar Maa on the elders' page. The letter page has a monogram of the couple's initials, the elders' page a baraat procession along the bottom, and the last page a pair of namaste hands.

Text is set in Hind Vadodara in warm brown, with the functions in two columns. The family page also carries a sweet note that names Shriji Maharaj and other Swaminarayan forms; it is typed text you can change. The painted deities are part of the artwork, so check that they suit your family before you pick this card.
