About this design

Rose Arch is a one-page painted engagement card, copied from a digital card. A cream marble archway on two columns fills the page, draped in pink roses, white blossoms and trailing ivy, with urns of roses on each column.

At the foot of the arch a watercolour couple stand at the top of a marble staircase, the groom in a grey suit and the bride in a long pale blue gown.

The names are set large in Great Vibes script in rose maroon, with the rest in Cormorant Garamond, inside the open arch.

Sample wording on this card

It opens with “together with our families”, then the two names with “and” between them, an invitation to join the engagement, the date as month, day and year, and the time and venue. Replace every name, date and address with your own before you share it. The card can also be switched to Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same page and artwork.
