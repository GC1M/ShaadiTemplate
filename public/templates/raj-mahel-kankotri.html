About this design

Raj Mahel is the longest kankotri on ShaadiTemplate: nine painted pages, one for every part of a Gujarati wedding, copied from a printed family set. The cover sets the tone with a couple seated on a sandstone palace balcony, seen from behind, under a cusped arch hung with brass lanterns, a peacock on the railing and hills in the evening haze. A small Ganesha, the શુભ વિવાહ title and the couple's monogram are lifted from the original artwork, so they keep its calligraphy.

Every page after that is its own scene. The letter page is a deep maroon arch with a painted doli carried by two bearers. The carnival page shows the couple in a garden by a fountain, the sangeet page a ballroom in midnight blue with chandeliers, and the mandap muhurat page a bride and groom seated before the sacred fire. The jaan page opens onto a palace and a floral mandap, the elders' page is framed by pillars, jasmine garlands and banana leaves, the cousins' page is a red arch with elephants, and the closing page has a chandelier, green drapes and kalash lamps.

The page headings (દિવ્ય આશિષ, આગમન ના અભિલાષી, પરિવાર ના પુષ્પો and the dress-code lines) are cut from the painted card too. The text you edit is set in Hind Vadodara in plum and green, switching to cream on the three dark pages. It suits Gujarati Hindu families who want every function on its own page and plan to share the card on WhatsApp, where each painting fills the screen.
