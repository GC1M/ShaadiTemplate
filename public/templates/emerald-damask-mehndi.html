About this design

An emerald green damask of star medallions on dark forest green, with painted pink roses, orange marigolds, jasmine and rosebuds spilling in from the top right and bottom left corners. In the bottom right a laughing cartoon bride in a sage dress raises both hands to show her mehndi.

The bride's name and Mehndi Ceremony are set in a large cream copperplate script with the date row and address in a cream serif. It is the playful sister of Rani Damask Mehndi and has no god or religious line.

Sample wording on this card

The sample reads You are invited to Aisha's Mehndi Ceremony, on a Monday at 8:00 PM, with a placeholder address. Change the name, date and venue in the editor. The card can also be switched to Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil in the editor, with the same layout and artwork.
