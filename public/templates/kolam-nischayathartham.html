About this design

A pale stone-grey card, like a swept threshold. Across the top is a strip of kaavi, the red-oxide colour painted on temple walls and doorsteps, with a row of small white kolams in rice-flour style drawn across it. From the bottom rises a large kambi kolam: interlocking looped lines drawn round a lattice of dots, in kaavi red. A faint grid of kaavi pulli dots, the dots a kolam is drawn on, covers the whole card, and a thin kaavi line frames it.

The emblem is the namam of Lord Venkateswara (Balaji) in a small roundel, under the invocation. The title is set large in kaavi red, the names in dark red and the rest of the text in brown and grey. Because this is an engagement, the bride's and groom's parents are named under each of them.

It suits Tamil families holding a nischayathartham in a kalyana mandapam. For the wedding itself, Korvai Silk Zari is a six-page Tamil wedding card with the same Balaji emblem.

Sample wording on this card

The card opens with ஓம் நமோ வேங்கடேசாய and the title நிச்சயதார்த்தம், then the line நிச்சயதார்த்த விழாவிற்கு அன்புடன் அழைக்கிறோம். The bride is written with சௌ. and the groom with சி., each with their parents' names, joined by உடன். Below come the date with the weekday, an evening time (மாலை 6:30 மணி), a sample kalyana mandapam in Mylapore, Chennai and அன்புடன் அழைப்போர் (the hosts) with a phone number.
