About this design

A pale, misty blue-grey card with a gothic rose window at the top: a round window of ruby red and sapphire blue glass petals set in thin gold tracery, with small gold and blue glass beads round the rim and a gold cross on a blue roundel at its centre. A double gold hairline frame runs round the card, and a small gold cross sits on a short gold rule at the bottom. The same window heads every page.

Holy Matrimony is set in large navy serif under the window, and the couple's names are in flowing navy script. Section headings are in small capitals and the rest is in a light book serif, with highlights in old gold. There is no separate invocation line: the cross in the window carries the faith of the card.

The six pages follow a Catholic church wedding in Mumbai rather than a Hindu programme. The functions page has a bridal shower hosted by the bridesmaids and a roce, the coconut milk blessing for the bride and groom, plus a short rhyme from the children. The wedding-day page has the nuptial mass at 4:00, the reception at 7:30 with the toast, cake and first dance, and dinner and dance from 8:30. The letter gives thanks to God and asks guests to join in prayer and celebration, the family page lists the families, grandparents, bridesmaids and best man, and the closing page asks guests to reply OK. It suits Christian families, especially Goan, Mangalorean and East Indian Catholic weddings.
