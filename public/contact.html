What we can help with

  • Problems with a card: text that does not fit, a script that looks wrong, or a download that fails. Tell us the design name, the language, and the phone or browser you used.
  • Wording questions: how to word a line for a ceremony or family custom we have not covered. Our guides answer the most common ones.
  • Design requests: a community, ceremony or sacred symbol you would like to see. Many of our designs started as requests.
  • Privacy: questions about what the site stores. See the privacy policy first; your invitation stays in your own browser.

Please do not email us invitation files containing other people's phone numbers or addresses unless we ask for them to fix a problem.
