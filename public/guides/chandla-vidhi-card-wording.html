Chandla vidhi card wording in Gujarati

Guide · By Ashok Lalakiya · Published 22 November 2026

A Gujarati chandla vidhi (ચાંદલા વિધિ) card is a short kankotri for the engagement. It opens with શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ, names the family deity, then the parents of the child whose family is sending the card, the couple, the other child's parents, the date and time, and ends with the ભોજન સમારંભ, the શુભ સ્થળ and the નિમંત્રક. Below is the full text of a printed card you can copy, line by line, with an English version.

What the ceremony is called

Many Gujarati families call the engagement chandla vidhi or simply chandlo, after the red chandlo (tilak) applied during the ceremony. Others say sagai (સગાઈ), and in some communities the engagement is known as gol dhana, after the jaggery and coriander seeds shared to mark it. The card wording is the same whichever name you print as the title; use the one your family says.

The order of a chandla vidhi card

  1. Invocation: || શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ || at the top, often with a small Ganesha.
  2. Title: ચાંદલા વિધિ or સગાઈ.
  3. Opening: સ્નેહી સ્વજન ("dear family and friends"), then સહર્ષ ખુશાલી સાથે જણાવવાનું કે ("we are delighted to share that").
  4. Blessing line: the kuldevi or the family's God, for example અમારા કુળદેવી શ્રી બ્રહ્માણી માતાજીની અસીમ કૃપાથી.
  5. Your side: the parents and your child, with the native place.
  6. The couple: both names large, often with a pair of rings between them instead of a word.
  7. Other side: the other child's parents and native place.
  8. Date: the Vikram Samvat year, month, paksh (sud or vad) and tithi, the weekday and the date, and the time.
  9. Invitation line, then the meal, the venue and the hosts.

The family sending the card names its own child first. On the groom's card it reads "...ના સુપુત્ર (son of)", the couple, then "...ના સુપુત્રી (daughter of)"; on the bride's card the order is reversed.

ચિ. રુશિતા
ચિ. આર્યન

Words you will see on every card

  • ચિ. (chiranjeevi): before the name of an unmarried son or daughter.
  • અ.સૌ. (akhand saubhagyavati): before the name of a married woman, here the mothers.
  • સુપુત્ર / સુપુત્રી: son / daughter of.
  • નિવાસી (હાલ સુરત): the family's native village, and where they live now.
  • સુદ / વદ: the bright and dark halves of the lunar month, before the tithi number. Check the tithi with your family priest or a panchang, so that it matches the date and weekday you print.
  • શોભામાં અભિવૃદ્ધિ કરશોજી: "please add to the grace of the occasion", the usual closing request.
