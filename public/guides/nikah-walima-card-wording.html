Nikah and Walima card wording

Guide · By Ashok Lalakiya · Published 16 November 2026

A Nikah card usually opens with the Bismillah, sometimes adds a short Quranic verse such as Surah Ar-Rum 30:21, then names the hosts, the bride (D/o her parents) and the groom (S/o his parents), and gives the date, the time (often "after Zuhr prayer") and the venue. The Walima card follows the same order but is shorter.

The Bismillah at the top

Almost every Muslim wedding card begins with the Bismillah, centred above everything else:

بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ
Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

Many families in India and Pakistan print 786 in its place, the number traditionally used to stand for the Bismillah. Some do this because printed cards are eventually thrown away and they prefer not to put Arabic from the Quran on them. Others print the Arabic in full. Both are common; follow what your elders prefer. A card sent only on WhatsApp avoids the disposal question for many families.

Quranic verses and duas families print

A single short verse under the Bismillah is common on the Nikah card. Pick one, not several, and print the reference with it. Arabic vowel marks often break in design software, so ask someone who reads Arabic to check the final proof.

Surah Ar-Rum 30:21, the verse most often printed on Nikah cards:

وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِّتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً ۚ إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَآيَاتٍ لِّقَوْمٍ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ
And among His signs is that He created for you spouses from among yourselves, so that you may find tranquillity in them, and He placed between you love and mercy. Surely in this are signs for people who reflect. (Surah Ar-Rum 30:21)

Many cards print only the first part, up to "love and mercy".

Surah An-Naba 78:8, short enough for a small card:

وَخَلَقْنَاكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا
And We created you in pairs. (Surah An-Naba 78:8)

Surah Al-Furqan 25:74, a dua for one's spouse and children:

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا
Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and our children the comfort of our eyes, and make us leaders of the righteous. (Surah Al-Furqan 25:74)

The dua for newlyweds, which the Prophet (peace be upon him) said to congratulate a newly married man, reported in Sunan Abu Dawud and Jami at-Tirmidhi:

بَارَكَ اللّٰهُ لَكَ وَبَارَكَ عَلَيْكَ وَجَمَعَ بَيْنَكُمَا فِي خَيْرٍ
Barakallahu laka wa baraka alaika wa jama'a bainakuma fi khair
May Allah bless you, shower His blessings upon you, and join you together in goodness.

Translations of the Quran vary in wording. If your family uses a particular translation, use its English rather than the one above.

Host lines for the Nikah card

In many South Asian families the Nikah is hosted by the bride's family and the card comes from her parents. Name the parents, then the couple, then the time and place:

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
Mr. & Mrs. Imran Khan
request the pleasure of your company at the Nikah of their daughter
Zoya
with
Ayaan
S/o Mrs. Farah & Mr. Salim Siddiqui
Insha'Allah on Sunday, 21 November 2027
Nikah after Zuhr prayer (1:30 PM) · Lunch to follow
The Leela Palace, Lake Pichola, Udaipur

A more formal opening, common on printed cards:

With the blessings of Almighty Allah
we solicit your gracious presence
on the auspicious occasion of the Nikah of our daughter
Zoya
with
Ayaan

If the groom's family sends its own card, swap the order: "at the Nikah of their son Ayaan with Zoya, D/o Mrs. Rukhsar & Mr. Imran Khan".

D/o and S/o lines

D/o means "daughter of" and S/o "son of". Older cards give only the father's name; many families now name both parents, as the ShaadiTemplate sample does. Some add the grandfather's name, which helps relatives place the family.

Zoya
D/o Mrs. Rukhsar & Mr. Imran Khan
Granddaughter of Marhoom Janab Yusuf Khan
Ayaan
S/o Mrs. Farah & Mr. Salim Siddiqui

Common honorifics: Janab (Mr.) and Mohtarma (Mrs.) on Urdu-flavoured cards, Haji or Alhaj for someone who has performed Hajj, and Marhoom (for a man) or Marhooma (for a woman) for a parent or grandparent who has passed away. Check these with the person or their family before printing.

Insha'Allah and other phrases

  • Insha'Allah ("if Allah wills") goes before a future date: "The Nikah will be solemnised, Insha'Allah, on Sunday, 21 November 2027."
  • Alhamdulillah ("praise be to Allah") opens an announcement: "Alhamdulillah, with the blessings of our elders, we are happy to invite you..."
  • Nikah Masnoon or Aqd-e-Masnoon means a Nikah performed according to the Sunnah. Many Urdu cards use it in place of the word Nikah.
  • Prayer times. "After Zuhr", "after Asr" or "after Maghrib" is the traditional way to give the Nikah time. Add the clock time in brackets for guests travelling from another city or who do not pray at that mosque.
  • Seating. If there is separate seating for ladies, say so in one short line, such as "Separate arrangements for ladies".

Nikah, Walima, Mehndi and Manjha: which card says what

A Muslim wedding often has three or four events, and many families send a separate card for each.

  • Nikah is the marriage contract itself. It carries the Bismillah, the verse if you use one, the full family lines and the prayer time.
  • Walima is the feast after the Nikah, a Sunnah, traditionally given by the groom's family. Many families now host it jointly. It is the event wider friends and colleagues attend, so the card is shorter, often in English, and usually skips the verse.
  • Mehndi is the henna evening for the bride, with dholak and songs. The card is informal.
  • Manjha (also Manja or Mayun) is the turmeric ceremony a few days before, held in many Hyderabadi and North Indian families. The bride wears yellow and close family attend.

Mr. & Mrs. Imran Khan and Mr. & Mrs. Salim Siddiqui
request the pleasure of your company at the Walima of
Zoya
D/o Mrs. Rukhsar & Mr. Imran Khan
&
Ayaan
S/o Mrs. Farah & Mr. Salim Siddiqui
Monday, 22 November 2027, 8:00 PM onwards
The Leela Palace, Lake Pichola, Udaipur

Mehndi and Manjha cards can be much lighter:

Dholak, songs and henna
Mehndi of Zoya
Saturday, 20 November 2027, 3:00 PM onwards
Khan residence
Join us for the Manjha of Zoya
Saturday, 20 November 2027, 10:00 AM
Please come dressed in yellow

An Urdu Nikah card

A short, standard Urdu card. Dukhtar-e-nek akhtar ("our virtuous daughter") and aqd-e-masnoon are phrases elders will expect to see. Use a proper Nastaliq font, because Urdu set in a plain Arabic font looks wrong to most Urdu readers.

بسم اللہ الرحمٰن الرحیم
دعوتِ نکاح
بفضلِ تعالیٰ ہماری دختر نیک اختر
عزیزہ زویا سلمہا
کا عقدِ مسنون
عزیزم ایان سلمہ
(ابنِ جناب سلیم صدیقی صاحب)
کے ساتھ ان شاء اللہ بتاریخ ۲۲ نومبر ۲۰۲۶ بروز اتوار بعد نمازِ ظہر قرار پایا ہے۔
آپ سے گزارش ہے کہ تشریف لا کر تقریب کو رونق بخشیں اور نوبیاہتا جوڑے کو اپنی دعاؤں سے نوازیں۔
المنتظر: عمران خان و اہلِ خانہ

In English: "In the name of Allah. Invitation to the Nikah. By the grace of Allah, the Nikah of our daughter Zoya with Ayaan, son of Janab Salim Siddiqui, is fixed, Insha'Allah, for Sunday 21 November 2027 after Zuhr prayer. Please grace the occasion and bless the couple with your duas. Awaiting you: Imran Khan and family." Add the venue as بمقام followed by the hall name and address.

RSVP and "with best compliments from"

End the card with a contact for questions. On a joint card, give one number both families can answer.

RSVP
Khan & Siddiqui Families
+91 98765 43210

Many cards also carry a "with best compliments from" block listing uncles, aunts and cousins. It is the place for relatives who are not hosting but want to be named.

With best compliments from
Mr. & Mrs. Shahid Khan
Mr. & Mrs. Arif Khan
and all relatives and friends

A line from the children of the family is a popular touch: "Mere Khalu ki shaadi mein zaroor aana!" ("Do come to my uncle's wedding!"), signed with their names.
