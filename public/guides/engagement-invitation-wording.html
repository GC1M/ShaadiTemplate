Engagement invitation wording

Guide · By Ashok Lalakiya · Published 22 November 2026

An engagement or ring ceremony card needs five things: who is inviting, the couple's names, the day and time, the venue, and a line asking guests to come. Decide first whether the card is from the couple or from the parents; everything else follows. Below are sample lines in English for each style, short enough for a one-page card or a WhatsApp image.

From the couple

Modern English cards often come from the couple themselves. These two are the sample wording of the Meadow Vows and Rose Arch cards:

Celebrate with us as we exchange rings
Aryan & Rushita
Your company is requested at our engagement
20 December 2026
At 4 o'clock in the afternoon
Sayaji Garden Lawns, Vadodara
Together with our families
Aryan and Rushita
cordially invite you to join our engagement on
17 January 2027
6 o'clock in the evening
The Rose Pavilion, Pune

Other first lines that work: "We said yes!", "Two hearts, one ring, and all of you", "Join us as we make it official".

From the parents

When the parents host, the card is usually from the family whose child's name comes first, often the bride's:

Mrs. Hansa & Mr. Ramesh Desai
request the pleasure of your company
at the engagement ceremony of their daughter
Rushita
with
Aryan
son of Mrs. Varsha & Mr. Bharat Virani
on Saturday, 23 January 2027 at 5 pm
Pal Farm, Simada Gam, Surat
Dinner to follow

Both families together:

With the blessings of our elders
the Desai and Virani families
invite you to the ring ceremony of
Rushita & Aryan

With a blessing at the top

Many Hindu families keep one line of invocation even on an English card: "Shri Ganeshaya Namah", "With the blessings of Lord Ganesha", or the family deity, as in "With the blessings of our kuldevi". The Lantern Canopy card carries a gold Ganesha above English wording. Sikh families open with Ik Onkar; see the Anand Karaj guide for roka and chunni lines.

Name the ceremony your family uses

The engagement has many names, and the title on the card is the one guests will recognise: sagai, ring ceremony, roka (Punjabi and North Indian), mangni, chandla vidhi or gol dhana (Gujarati), sakharpuda (Marathi), nischayathartham (Tamil) and nishchitartham (Telugu). For Gujarati wording in Gujarati script, see chandla vidhi card wording; for Tamil, the Tamil invitation guide covers the nischayathartham card.

Ring Ceremony
Please join us for the sagai of
Rushita & Aryan

Sagai and roka card in Hindi

A Hindi sagai card follows the shaadi card: ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥, the hosts, the son or daughter as चि. (chiranjeevi) and the bride as सौ. कां. (saubhagyakankshini), then the date, time and venue. This one is from the groom's family:

॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥
सगाई समारोह
श्रीमती सुनीता एवं श्री राजेश शर्मा
अपने सुपुत्र
चि. आर्यन
की सगाई
सौ. कां. रुशिता
(सुपुत्री श्रीमती हंसा एवं श्री रमेश देसाई)
के साथ होनी निश्चित हुई है।
इस शुभ अवसर पर आप सपरिवार सादर आमंत्रित हैं।
दिनांक: शनिवार, 23 जनवरी 2027
समय: सायं 5 बजे
स्थान: होटल का नाम और पता

For a roka, which is often smaller and held at home, one short block is enough:

रोका समारोह
चि. आर्यन एवं सौ. कां. रुशिता के रोके के शुभ अवसर पर
आप सपरिवार सादर आमंत्रित हैं।

For the full Hindi card format, see Hindi shadi card matter.

Short lines for WhatsApp

Send the card image with a line of text, since the message preview shows the text first:

We are getting engaged! Rushita and Aryan's ring ceremony is on Saturday, 23 January at 5 pm, at Pal Farm, Surat. The card is attached. We would love to see you there.
With great joy we invite you and your family to Rushita and Aryan's engagement. Details are on the card. Please bless the couple with your presence.

RSVP and dress lines

  • "Kindly confirm by 10 January: Hansa Desai, 98765 43210"
  • "Dinner to follow" or "Followed by dinner and dance"
  • "Dress code: Indian festive" or "Colours of the evening: blush and gold"
  • "Your blessings are the only gift we need"
