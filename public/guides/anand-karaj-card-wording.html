Anand Karaj invitation wording

Guide · By Ashok Lalakiya · Published 16 November 2027

An Anand Karaj card opens with ੴ (Ik Onkar), often as ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ, may add one line of Gurbani, then names the parents and the couple and lists the morning programme: the milni, the Anand Karaj and laavan at the Gurdwara Sahib, and langar or lunch. Below are lines in Punjabi and English you can copy.

The invocation at the top

The first line of a Sikh wedding card is almost always ੴ, Ik Onkar, "there is one Creator". Families choose how much to print after it:

ੴ
ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥
ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ

ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ (Ik Onkar Satgur Prasad) means "One Creator, realised by the grace of the True Guru". ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ (Ik Onkar Sat Naam) means "One Creator, whose name is Truth"; it is what the ShaadiTemplate editor prints by default. Some families print the whole Mool Mantar, the opening of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji (Ang 1):

ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਅਜੂਨੀ ਸੈਭੰ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥
One Creator. Truth is the Name. The Creator, without fear, without enmity, timeless in form, beyond birth, self-existent, known by the Guru's grace.

A letter-style card often opens with the Sikh greeting instead of, or below, the invocation:

ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਜੀ ਕਾ ਖ਼ਾਲਸਾ ॥ ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਜੀ ਕੀ ਫ਼ਤਹਿ ॥

Because printed cards are eventually thrown away, some families print only ੴ and leave longer Gurbani off the card. Others print it and ask relatives to treat the card with respect. Follow your family's practice.

Gurbani lines often printed on cards

If you add a line of Gurbani, use one, with its English meaning and Ang (page) number. Gurmukhi in Gurbani carries vowel signs such as the aunkar in ਪਿਰੁ that are not used in modern Punjabi spelling. Do not let a typesetter "correct" them.

The line most associated with Sikh marriage, by Guru Amar Das Ji (Ang 788):

ਧਨ ਪਿਰੁ ਏਹਿ ਨ ਆਖੀਅਨਿ ਬਹਨਿ ਇਕਠੇ ਹੋਇ ॥
ਏਕ ਜੋਤਿ ਦੁਇ ਮੂਰਤੀ ਧਨ ਪਿਰੁ ਕਹੀਐ ਸੋਇ ॥
They are not called husband and wife who merely sit together. They alone are called husband and wife who have one light in two bodies.

A line about beginning any undertaking with the Guru's blessing, printed on many cards for the Anand Karaj and other family occasions (Sri Raag, Ang 91):

ਕੀਤਾ ਲੋੜੀਐ ਕੰਮੁ ਸੁ ਹਰਿ ਪਹਿ ਆਖੀਐ ॥
ਕਾਰਜੁ ਦੇਇ ਸਵਾਰਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਚੁ ਸਾਖੀਐ ॥
Whatever task you wish to accomplish, place it before the Lord. The True Guru gives this true testimony: He will bring your task to completion.

The wedding day programme

The Anand Karaj is usually held in the morning at the Gurdwara Sahib, so the card is built around a morning schedule:

  • Milni (ਮਿਲਣੀ): the two families meet when the baraat arrives, usually after a short Ardas. Relatives of the same relation (the two fathers, the two maternal uncles, and so on) embrace and exchange garlands or shawls.
  • Tea and breakfast (ਚਾਹ-ਪਾਣੀ) after the milni.
  • Anand Karaj in the presence of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji. The couple walk around Guru Granth Sahib Ji four times as the four laavan (ਲਾਵਾਂ), the verses by Guru Ram Das Ji in Raag Suhi, are read and sung. The ceremony ends with Ardas.
  • Langar or lunch: ਲੰਗਰ if guests eat in the Gurdwara hall, or ਪ੍ਰੀਤੀ ਭੋਜ if lunch is at a separate venue.

Many families add a line reminding guests to cover their heads and remove their shoes in the Gurdwara Sahib, which helps guests from outside the community.

Milni: 8:30 AM
Tea and breakfast: 9:00 AM
Anand Karaj: 10:00 AM
Lunch: 12:30 PM onwards
Gurdwara Sahib Sri Guru Singh Sabha, Model Town, Ludhiana
ਮਿਲਣੀ: ਸਵੇਰੇ 8:30 ਵਜੇ
ਲਾਵਾਂ ਤੇ ਆਨੰਦ ਕਾਰਜ: ਸਵੇਰੇ 10:00 ਵਜੇ
ਸ੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਗ੍ਰੰਥ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਜੀ ਦੀ ਹਜ਼ੂਰੀ ਵਿੱਚ
ਪ੍ਰੀਤੀ ਭੋਜ: ਦੁਪਹਿਰ 12:30 ਵਜੇ ਤੋਂ
ਸਥਾਨ: ਗੁਰਦੁਆਰਾ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਸਿੰਘ ਸਭਾ, ਮਾਡਲ ਟਾਊਨ, ਲੁਧਿਆਣਾ

Family host lines in English

The card traditionally comes from the bride's parents. Write Singh and Kaur in full, and use Sardar and Sardarni (or S. and Sdn.) consistently for everyone on the card.

Ik Onkar
With the blessings of Waheguru and our elders
Sardarni Sukhwinder Kaur & Sardar Jaspal Singh Gill
request the pleasure of your company with family
at the Anand Karaj of their daughter
Simran Kaur
with
Harpreet Singh
son of Sardarni Narinder Kaur & Sardar Gurmeet Singh Sandhu
on Sunday, 21 November 2027

Other openings families use: "By the grace of Waheguru", or "With Waheguru's kirpa and the blessings of our elders". On the groom's family card, reverse the names: "at the Anand Karaj of their son Harpreet Singh with Simran Kaur, daughter of Sardarni Sukhwinder Kaur & Sardar Jaspal Singh Gill".

The reception

The reception is usually in the evening, at a banquet hall or hotel rather than the Gurdwara, and is often hosted by the groom's family or by both families. Many families send it in English because colleagues and friends are invited.

The Gill and Sandhu families request the pleasure of your company
at the reception of
Simran Kaur & Harpreet Singh
Monday, 22 November 2027, 8:00 PM onwards
Dinner and celebrations
ਪ੍ਰੀਤੀ ਭੋਜ
ਗਿੱਲ ਅਤੇ ਸੰਧੂ ਪਰਿਵਾਰ ਨਵ-ਵਿਆਹੁਤਾ ਜੋੜੀ ਦੇ ਪ੍ਰੀਤੀ ਭੋਜ ਵਿੱਚ ਆਪ ਜੀ ਦੀ ਹਾਜ਼ਰੀ ਦੇ ਚਾਹਵਾਨ ਹਨ

Pre-wedding events and their cards

A Punjabi wedding has several smaller functions in the week before. Practice varies a lot between families and regions, so use the names your family uses.

  • Roka (ਰੋਕਾ): the first formal step, when the families agree to the match and exchange gifts and sweets. It is often small and arranged by phone, but some families send a card.
  • Chunni chadana (ਚੁੰਨੀ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਉਣਾ): the groom's family drapes a red chunni over the bride's head, often together with the engagement (ਕੁੜਮਾਈ or ਮੰਗਣੀ).
  • Path: many families hold a Sukhmani Sahib path or an Akhand Path at home or the Gurdwara before the wedding, and list it on the main card.
  • Maiyan or Vatna (ਮਾਈਆਂ, ਵਟਣਾ): a paste of turmeric, flour and oil is applied to the bride and groom, each at their own home, while the women of the family sing.
  • Mehndi (ਮਹਿੰਦੀ): the henna evening, often with giddha.
  • Jaago (ਜਾਗੋ): usually the night before the wedding. The maternal relatives (nanke) carry a decorated pot with lit lamps through the neighbourhood, dancing and singing boliyan.

Many families combine the Maiyan (often spelt Mayun), Mehndi and Jaago into one evening and send a single card for it:

Mayun, Mehndi & Jaago
Come dance with the nanke as we carry the jaago through the street
Saturday, 20 November 2027, 7:30 PM onwards
Gill residence, Model Town, Ludhiana
ਮਹਿੰਦੀ ਦੀ ਰਸਮ
ਮਹਿੰਦੀ ਦੀ ਰੰਗਲੀ ਸ਼ਾਮ ਵਿੱਚ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਪਧਾਰਨਾ ਜੀ
20/11/2027, ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ, ਦੁਪਹਿਰ 3:00 ਵਜੇ
ਜਾਗੋ
ਨਾਨਕੇ ਮੇਲ ਨਾਲ ਜਾਗੋ ਕੱਢਣ ਲਈ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਪਧਾਰਨਾ ਜੀ
20/11/2027, ਸ਼ਨੀਵਾਰ, ਰਾਤ 9:00 ਵਜੇ

For a Roka or engagement card, a single line is enough:

ਮੰਗਣੀ ਦੀ ਰਸਮ
ਮੰਗਣੀ ਦੀ ਸ਼ੁਭ ਰਸਮ ਮੌਕੇ ਆਪ ਜੀ ਨੂੰ ਪਿਆਰ ਭਰਿਆ ਸੱਦਾ
14/11/2027, ਐਤਵਾਰ, ਸ਼ਾਮ 6:30 ਵਜੇ
