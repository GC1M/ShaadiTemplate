Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use ShaadiTemplate (the "site"). The short version: there is no account, and your invitation stays in your own browser.

Your invitation

The names, dates, venues and photos you enter are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes your draft.

Phonetic typing

When you use phonetic typing for an Indian language, each word you type is sent to Google Input Tools (inputtools.google.com) to convert it into that script. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

Sample couple websites

The sample wedding websites load images from Wikimedia Commons. That service receives your IP address and browser details when it serves that content, under its own privacy policy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used: which pages and designs people open, which country and device they visit from, how long they stay, whether downloads succeed, and which errors happen. Clarity records clicks, scrolling and mouse movement so we can fix confusing parts of the editor; text you type into the editor is masked and not recorded. Neither tool receives your invitation file. Both use cookies; see how Google uses data and the Microsoft privacy statement.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads served by Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Payments

If you buy a paid feature, the payment is handled by our payment provider (such as Razorpay). We receive your name, email, phone number and the payment status. We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions about privacy: hello@shaaditemplate.com.
