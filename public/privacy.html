Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use ShaadiTemplate (the "site"). The short version: there is no account, your invitation card stays in your own browser, and a wedding website is stored on our servers only when you publish it.

Your invitation

The names, dates, venues and photos you enter are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes your draft.

Wedding websites

While you fill in a wedding website, your draft is kept in your browser. When you press Publish, the details and photos you entered (names, families, events, venue, story, notes and photos) are stored on our servers, run by Cloudflare, and shown to anyone who has the site's link. Published sites are not listed by search engines, but anyone you share the link with can pass it on, so only add what you are happy for guests to see.

Guests who reply on a wedding website give their name, which side they are with, which events they will attend, how many are coming and an optional note. Replies are shown only to the couple, through their edit link. Blessings (a name and a message) are shown to everyone who visits the site. Reports sent with "Report this site" are seen only by us.

To stop abuse, we keep a scrambled (salted and hashed) form of your IP address with each published site, reply, blessing and report, and use it only to limit how often one connection can post. We do not keep your IP address itself.

When publishing, the couple may give an email address and a WhatsApp number, and say how they found us. All are optional. With the site we also keep where the couple first came to ShaadiTemplate from (the website or link that sent them, or "direct"), which your browser remembers from your first visit; it helps us see which places bring couples, and it goes when the site is deleted. We use the email to send the edit link, a daily note when guests reply, and a reminder before the site is deleted, and we send these emails through our email provider, Resend. The WhatsApp number is only stored, so we can reach you about your site. We do not use either for anything else, and we never sell or share them, unless you also tick "send me new designs and offers": then we may email you about new designs now and then, and every such email lets you stop them. Your contact details are deleted with your site, except when you ticked that box; write to us at any time to have them removed.

The couple can change or delete their site at any time with their edit link; deleting it removes its photos, replies and blessings. If you lose the edit link or want something removed from a site, write to us. A site is deleted automatically 30 days after its last event, with its photos, replies and blessings; the editor shows the date. We also remove sites that break our terms.

Phonetic typing

When you use phonetic typing for an Indian language, each word you type is sent to Google Input Tools (inputtools.google.com) to convert it into that script. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

Sample couple websites

The sample wedding websites load images from Wikimedia Commons. That service receives your IP address and browser details when it serves that content, under its own privacy policy.

Music on wedding websites

When a wedding website plays a song from YouTube (the sample sites, and any site whose couple picked a YouTube song), the song plays in YouTube's player from youtube-nocookie.com once you tap the page or "Play our song". YouTube then receives your IP address and browser details under Google's privacy policy.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how the site is used: which pages and designs people open, which country and device they visit from, how long they stay, whether downloads succeed, and which errors happen. Clarity records clicks, scrolling and mouse movement so we can fix confusing parts of the editor; text you type into the editor is masked and not recorded. Neither tool receives your invitation file. Both use cookies; see how Google uses data and the Microsoft privacy statement.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads served by Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Payments

If you buy a paid feature, the payment is handled by our payment provider (such as Razorpay). We receive your name, email, phone number and the payment status. We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions about privacy: hello@shaaditemplate.com.
