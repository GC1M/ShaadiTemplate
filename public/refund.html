Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Most of ShaadiTemplate is free. This policy covers paid digital features such as premium designs or high-resolution downloads.

Refunds

Because paid features are digital and available immediately, purchases are generally not refundable. We will give a full refund if:

  • you were charged but the feature did not unlock or the download did not work, and we cannot fix it;
  • you were charged twice for the same purchase.

Email hello@shaaditemplate.com within 7 days of the payment with your payment ID. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Cancellation

Purchases are one-time payments, not subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel. You can stop using the site at any time.

Delivery

Paid features are delivered online, right after payment. Nothing is shipped.
